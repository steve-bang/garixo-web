## The Real Cost of Staying on Spreadsheets

Let’s look at practical examples.

### Lost Labor Hours

If a technician forgets to log 1 billable hour per day and no system flags it:

- 5 hours per week
- 20 hours per month
- At $120/hour = $2,400/month lost

That’s not small — and it's the same pattern that shows up across [the hidden cost of running an auto repair shop on paper](https://www.garixo.com/blog/cost-of-paper-based-auto-repair-shop), whether the tracking method is a clipboard or a spreadsheet.

### Underpriced Parts

If markup formulas aren’t consistent, you may:

- Undershoot target margin
- Forget shop supplies
- Round incorrectly

Even a 5% margin leak on $50,000 in parts sales is real money.

### Delayed Invoices

If billing isn’t streamlined:

- Invoices go out late
- Payments come in late
- Cash flow tightens

Spreadsheets don’t automate any of that.
