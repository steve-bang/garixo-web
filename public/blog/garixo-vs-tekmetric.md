**Quick answer:** Garixo and Tekmetric both manage work orders, invoicing, and customer records for auto repair shops, but they're built around different priorities. Tekmetric leans toward reporting depth and repair-data integration — strong for shops that manage technician performance and job profitability through detailed analytics. Garixo leans toward simplicity and flat pricing for independent shops with 1–10 technicians who want the core workflow running fast, without a longer setup process. Which one is "better" depends on how deep you actually need to go with reporting.

I'm Steve, Garixo's founder, so I'll say upfront where my bias sits. But this is meant to help you decide, not to talk you out of Tekmetric if it's genuinely the better fit for your shop.

---

## Where the Two Platforms Actually Differ

### Reporting and Repair-Data Integration

This is Tekmetric's clearest strength. It's built for shops that want to dig into the numbers — technician efficiency, job profitability, labor-time benchmarks tied to repair data. If your shop is managed heavily through dashboards and detailed performance metrics, this is where Tekmetric tends to shine.

Garixo covers the reporting a small shop actually checks day to day — revenue, job status, basic performance visibility — but it isn't built around the same depth of analytics Tekmetric offers. If granular reporting is a must-have for how you run the shop, it's worth testing that specifically during a trial rather than assuming either platform goes as deep as you picture.

### Setup and Learning Curve

Garixo is built specifically for small independent shops, and that shows up most in setup time — most shops are running real jobs within a day, and a new service writer can learn the basics in an afternoon. Tekmetric's broader analytics and reporting capability generally means more configuration up front to get everything tuned the way you want it.

Neither is objectively "too complicated" — it's a question of whether you want more configuration in exchange for deeper reporting, or a faster path to a simple daily workflow. If you're not sure your shop needs a dedicated platform at all yet, [10 signs your auto repair shop is ready for management software](https://www.garixo.com/blog/signs-auto-repair-shop-needs-management-software) is worth a read first.

### Pricing Approach

Garixo publishes flat-rate pricing starting at **$79/month**, with no per-user fees — the bill doesn't change when you add a technician or a second device. Tekmetric's pricing is generally quote-based and scales with shop size and feature tier, so the only accurate way to compare real cost is to request current pricing directly from Tekmetric for your specific shop.

### Who Each One Is Built For

Garixo is built for independent shops with 1–10 technicians who want work orders, invoicing, inventory, and scheduling running well in one simple, flat-rate platform. See the full [Garixo features](https://www.garixo.com/features) breakdown.

Tekmetric tends to suit shops that want to manage the business through detailed analytics and are comfortable with more setup to get a deeper reporting capability tuned in.

## A Practical Way to Decide

Rather than picking from a comparison article, the more reliable test is:

1. **Name the one thing that would make you switch today.** If it's "I can't see technician efficiency or job profitability clearly enough," that points toward Tekmetric's strength. If it's "our setup needs to be painless and our pricing needs to stay predictable as we grow," that points toward what Garixo is built for.
2. **Trial both on real jobs**, not sample data — a demo environment won't show you the friction (or lack of it) that shows up on an actual busy day.
3. **Time your own onboarding.** If your service writer isn't comfortable within the first day or two, that's a real cost even though it's not on the invoice.

If missed charges or slow invoicing are part of what's pushing the decision, [why your shop is losing $40,000 a year in unbilled labor](https://www.garixo.com/blog/unbilled-labor-auto-repair-shop) is worth reading alongside whichever platform you're testing — the fix matters more than which dashboard it shows up on.

If detailed reporting is a top priority, that's worth testing directly in each platform's trial.

### Is Tekmetric more expensive than Garixo?

Garixo publishes flat-rate pricing starting at $79/month with no per-user fees. Tekmetric's pricing is generally quote-based, so the only accurate way to compare total cost is to request current pricing directly from Tekmetric for your shop's size.

### Which shops is Garixo a better fit for than Tekmetric?

Independent shops with 1–10 technicians who want the simplest possible setup and predictable flat pricing tend to be the best fit for Garixo. Shops that lean heavily on detailed analytics and repair-data integration may prefer Tekmetric's approach.
